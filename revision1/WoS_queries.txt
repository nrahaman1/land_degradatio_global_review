Web of Science Core Collection: replication of Table 1 queries (Reviewer comment i)
================================================================================
Where: lib.vt.edu > Databases > Web of Science (sign in with VT credentials) > Advanced Search
TS = Topic (title, abstract, author keywords, Keywords Plus); the closest match to Scopus TITLE-ABS-KEY.

Run each query SEPARATELY, then before exporting apply these filters (left panel):
  - Document Types: Article, Proceedings Paper (Early Access is fine)
  - Languages: English
  - Publication Years: 1982 to 2025 only (excludes 2026)

Export: Export > Tab delimited file > Record Content = "Full Record" > records 1-1000
(If a query has more than 1000 results, export 1001-2000 etc. as a separate file.)
Save each query's file(s) (e.g. 1-1000.txt, 1001-2000.txt) into its own folder:
  Revision_1/WoS_Data/Q01_Drivers_Human/ ... Revision_1/WoS_Data/Q11_Feedback/  (folder number = query number below)

Q01 Drivers / Human (excl.)     TS=("land degradation" AND drivers AND human AND factors NOT natural)
Q02 Drivers / Natural (excl.)   TS=("land degradation" AND drivers AND natural AND factors NOT human)
Q03 Drivers / Combined          TS=("land degradation" AND drivers AND natural AND factors AND human)
Q04 Processes / Physical        TS=("land degradation" AND process AND physical)
Q05 Processes / Chemical        TS=("land degradation" AND process AND chemical)
Q06 Processes / Biological      TS=("land degradation" AND process AND biological)
Q07 Processes / Mixed           TS=("land degradation" AND process AND biological AND chemical AND physical)
Q08 Impacts / Environmental     TS=("land degradation" AND impact AND environmental NOT socioeconomic)
Q09 Impacts / Socioeconomic     TS=("land degradation" AND impact AND socioeconomic NOT environmental)
Q10 Impacts / Combined          TS=("land degradation" AND impact AND socioeconomic AND environmental)
Q11 Feedback                    TS=("land degradation" AND feedback AND loop)
